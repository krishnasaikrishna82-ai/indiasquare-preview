About IndiaSquare

Built by merchants, carried by the community.

IndiaSquare is promoted by the Jersey City Asian Merchant Association (JCAMA) and Journal Square Restoration Corporation, with Govinda Sanskar Center. What began as merchants improving daily life in the business district grew into a cultural landmark.
