JCAMA · Govinda Sanskar Center · JSRC

Built by merchants, carried by community

The Jersey City Asian Merchant Association came together to solve everyday quality-of-life issues in the city’s business district — and grew into one of the region’s great cultural celebrations.

Our story

From a merchants’ group to IndiaSquare

JCAMA is a group of New Jersey merchants who have been making a difference on Newark Avenue for well over a decade. It began with the day-to-day problems of a busy business district — and a shared belief that this stretch of Jersey City could be the area’s most vibrant business hub.

The group widened its work to scholarships for students, support for one-teacher schools in India, area community colleges and hospitals, and building understanding between the many communities who share the city. Staying strictly non-political, JCAMA also encourages everyone to take part in elections and vote.

Navratri on IndiaSquare started as a cultural idea from JCAMA. With Govinda Sanskar Center, Journal Square Restoration Corporation, the City of Jersey City and the Urban Enterprise Zone of New Jersey, it now welcomes about 100,000 participants and visitors over four nights.
